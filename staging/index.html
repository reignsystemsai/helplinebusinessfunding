What type of funding is this?

HelpLine focuses on merchant cash advances (MCAs). An MCA provides upfront funding in exchange for an agreed amount of future business revenue. It differs from a traditional bank loan.

Do I need all the bank paperwork?

You can start this inquiry with basic business information. A provider may later request recent business bank statements and additional documents. Requirements vary by provider and offer.

Can I apply if a bank turned me down?

You can request a review. Alternative providers may assess your business differently, but a bank decline does not mean you will qualify for an MCA.

How much will funding cost?

The total cost and collection frequency depend on the offer. MCA costs can be higher than traditional loan costs, and daily or weekly collections can affect cash flow. Review the full agreement and any fees or guarantees before accepting.

Will this inquiry obligate me to accept funding?

No. Submitting this form requests follow-up about funding options. It does not create a financing agreement or guarantee an offer.

How we use your information

We collect the contact and business information you submit to review your funding inquiry and contact you about it. Submissions are stored in our Supabase database and accessed by the HelpLine team.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, passwords, or bank statements through this form. Any additional documentation will be requested separately.
